# National Security Archives

Typically, NSA staff contact RDG with a request for a record to be added to our ILS. They will provide a document with basic information (this may or may not include MARC tagging).

Ask staff at NSA:

1. If they wish the record we create to be in OCLC or not
   1. If record is added to OCLC capture that # and provide to NSA staff via email
2. If they wish the record we create to be in OCLC or not
   1. If sent to WRLC capture holdings MMSID and provide to NSA staff and WRLC staff via a WRLC support ticket

## Bibliographic record

* Review information provided. Use authorized LCSH forms for any subject headings provided
* Change 710 2/ National Security Archive (U.S.) to 978 1/ National Security Archive (U.S.)

## Holdings record

* Create and suppress holdings record.
* For materials going to SCF holdings location is:
  * 852 0/ $b gelman $h wrlc snsa
* 866 may be required:
  * 866 41 $8 0 $a Boxes X-XX
* 561 should be included:
  * 561 1/ National Security Archive (U.S.)
