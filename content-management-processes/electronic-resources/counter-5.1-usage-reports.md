# COUNTER 5.1 Usage Reports

COUNTER 5.1 usage statistics are the current standard for Counter usage statistics. To be compliant, a  Master Platform report (at a minimum) must be provided however, four master reports and standard reports are typically available.\* The [COUNTER Registry](https://registry.countermetrics.org/) details which vendor/providers are Counter 5.0/5.1 compliant and a list of the reports each vendor provides.

\*Although all four master reports may be offered, all four reports may not generate statistics based on the content of our subscription(s) on any one platform.

**Master and Standard Reports**

Master Reports provide comprehensive information on usage by vendor. In-depth explanations of each report are available at [countermetrics.org](https://www.countermetrics.org/) by clicking the links below:

[Master Platform Reports:](https://cop5.countermetrics.org/en/5.1.0.1/04-reports/01-platform-reports.html) Summarizes activity on a single platform (vendor).

[Master Database Reports:](https://cop5.countermetrics.org/en/5.1.0.1/04-reports/02-database-reports.html) Summarizes activity for a database or materials packaged like a database.

[Master Title Reports:](https://cop5.countermetrics.org/en/5.1.0.1/04-reports/03-title-reports.html) Summarizes activity at the title level (books and journals)

[Master Item Reports:](https://cop5.countermetrics.org/en/5.1.0.1/04-reports/04-item-reports.html) Summarizes activity at the item (article or multimedia item)

**Standard Reports:** There are twelve standard reports, each a subset of one of the Master Reports. Best practices suggest downloading master reports rather than standard reports because they provide comprehensive data and can easily be filtered for more specific data.

Common standard reports include:

&#x20;   **PR\_P1:** platform usage

&#x20;   **DR\_D1:** Database Search and Item Usage

&#x20;     **TR\_J1:** Journal Requests (Controlled)

&#x20;     **TR\_B1:** Books Requests (Controlled)

&#x20;     **IR\_M1:** Multimedia Item Requests

&#x20;

Additional information can be found on COUNTERmetrics.org:

Friendly Guide: [Reports: Four Reports, Multiple Standard Views](https://www.countermetrics.org/education/reports/).

**Elements of a COUNTER Report**

1. **Report Header**
2. **Column Headings**: report specific
3. **Filters and Attributes** are specific to each report and may include Data\_Type, Access Type, Access\_Method, and Metric\_Type. Specific information for each report can be found by clicking on the links below.

[Platform Reports](https://cop5.countermetrics.org/en/5.1.0.1/04-reports/01-platform-reports.html#filters-and-attributes)

[Database Reports](https://cop5.countermetrics.org/en/5.1.0.1/04-reports/02-database-reports.html#filters-and-attributes)

[Title Reports](https://cop5.countermetrics.org/en/5.1.0.1/04-reports/03-title-reports.html#filters-and-attributes)

[Item Reports](https://cop5.countermetrics.org/en/5.1.0.1/04-reports/04-item-reports.html#filters-and-attributes)

**Transitioning from Counter 5.0 to 5.1**

Most vendors and providers now provide Counter 5.1; some vendors remain that only provide Counter 5 statistics. [Section 13 Transitioning from Previous Releases or to New Reporting Services](https://cop5.countermetrics.org/en/5.1.0.1/13-transitioning/#transitioning-from-previous-releases-or-to-new-reporting-services) offers information on the comparison between Counter 5.0 and Counter 5.1 reports.

The technical details on Counter 5.1 can be found at [COUNTER Code of Practice Release 5.1.0.1](https://cop5.countermetrics.org/en/5.1.0.1/index.html) on the counter metrics website.
