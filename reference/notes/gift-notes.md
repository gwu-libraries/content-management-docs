# Gift notes

Gift notes go into a local 975 field (indicators 1\_) in the bib record and the 541 (indicators 1\_) in holdings.&#x20;

* $a - Gifter's name
* $c - Gift of / donation by / From the library of heading, to be immediately followed by a colon space $a
  * $c Gift of: $a Bilbo Baggins
* $d - Date (when applicable)
* $e - Acession number (when applicable)
