## GRC Serials Binding Process

This is similar to the stacks process, above, but involves GRC staff in the initial steps and is decoupled from decisions on what to send to the WRLC Shared Collections Facility. All GRC serials return to GRC stacks after being bound.



1. [Director of Global Resources selects title to be bound (GRC)](../../partners/global-resources-center/grc-serials-holdings-policy/serials-binding-grc-steps.md)
2. [Prepare bindable units (GRC)](../../partners/global-resources-center/grc-serials-holdings-policy/serials-binding-grc-steps.md#step-2-prepare-bindable-units)
3. [Preliminary Bindery Prep (GRC)](../../partners/global-resources-center/grc-serials-holdings-policy/serials-binding-grc-steps.md#step-3-preliminary-bindery-prep)
4. [Final Bindery Prep (MonoAcq)](../../content-management-processes/bindery/serials-binding-monoacq-steps.md#docs-internal-guid-06d35ff4-7fff-f045-2413-1b5a4dbfd342)
