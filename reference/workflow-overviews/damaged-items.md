# Damaged Items Overview

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Content Management physical workflow overviews-Damaged (New).drawio(2).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Damaged book workflow flow chart</p></figcaption></figure>

* [Circulation sends damaged items to Content Management](../../partners/access-services/damaged-items.md)
  * Fill out damaged slip
  * Place items on "Incoming Damaged" shelf
* [Content Management scans in items](https://app.gitbook.com/s/5QX2M9ayhWC5XrrMkvz8/content-management-processes)
  * Use the damaged item decision tree steps to determine the action needed
  * Set status to "Damaged - CDL Review", "Damaged - Rebind", "Damaged - Replace", or "Withdraw"
  * Place on appropriate shelf
* [Collection Development Librarians review items on the "CDL Review" shelves](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1x6sYK7StgDsNTp7weLkYmCu5NCJIfuvWtxjjPk0HN4U/edit?usp=sharing)
  * Once decisions are made, items are placed on the "CDL Rebind", "CDL Replace", or "CDL Withdraw" shelves
* Content Management scans CDL decision items to set new status and places items on appropriate shelf
* Items marked for replacement are replaced
* Items marked for rebinding are sent to the bindery
* Items marked for withdrawal are withdrawn
